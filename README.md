# 2026 BIC Back-to-School (BTS) In-Store Activation Dashboard

**Project Category:** Business Intelligence | Sales Analytics | Marketing Performance Measurement

**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power BI

### Project Overview

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to monitor the performance of the 2026 BIC Back-to-School In-Store Activation across six cities and 96 active outlets. The dashboard consolidates sales performance, bundle distribution, revenue achievement, gift item utilisation, and weekly sales trends into a centralised reporting solution.

The dashboard enables stakeholders to assess campaign performance against targets, identify high- and low-performing locations, monitor promotional bundle uptake, and track sales trends throughout the activation period.

### Key Performance Highlights

* **₦163.91M** in total sales value recorded, achieving 107% of the target.
* **14,859 bundles** sold, representing 106% of the target.
* **64,068 consumers reached** across six cities and 96 active outlets.
* **Lagos Mainland** led bundle sales volume with 4,831 bundles achieved against a target of 4,285.
* **Gift utilisation tracking** provided visibility into the distribution of notebooks, water bottles, and school bags.

### Dashboard Capabilities

* KPI tracking for total bundles sold, sales value, and target achievement.
* Bundle distribution analysis across Bronze, Silver, and Gold categories.
* Revenue performance comparison by bundle category.
* Gift item utilisation monitoring.
* City-level target versus actual sales analysis.
* Weekly sales volume trend analysis.
* Interactive filtering by week and city.

### Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of activation performance, helping stakeholders monitor target achievement, evaluate geographical performance, identify sales gaps, and make informed decisions on field execution and promotional resource allocation.
